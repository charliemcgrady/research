6 runs · 120 experiments. The hypothesis was refuted in its stated direction: CIN tracks an immune-cold state (checkpoint-low), not driver-specific, not TIGIT/LAG3-selective, not cGAS-STING-mediated. STK11 loss emerged as a focal contributor to coldness.

asta analyze-data submit "In driver-negative LUAD, re-test CIN → LAG3 per T cell using
