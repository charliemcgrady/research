# Chromosomal instability, immune escape, and LAG3 in oncogene-negative lung adenocarcinoma
### An Asta-driven hypothesis-generation → validation project — summary for the driver-negative LUAD investigator

---

## 1. Why we did this

Oncogene-negative ("driver-negative", RTK/RAS/RAF pathway wild-type) LUAD remains the enigmatic
tail of the disease: ~15–20% of adenocarcinomas with no actionable driver, poorly served by targeted
therapy and variably by immunotherapy. Our entry point was a mechanistic hypothesis generated by the
**Asta Theorizer**: that in these tumors **chromosomal instability (CIN) is the oncogenic engine, and
that CIN drives immune escape** — with the second-generation checkpoints **TIGIT and LAG3** as the
proposed effectors (panel-aware against PD-1/PD-L1/TIM-3/CTLA-4).

The question we set out to answer: *is that mechanism real and specific to driver-negative LUAD, and
does it point to a therapeutic axis (e.g. LAG3 blockade) for a group that badly needs one?*

Starting data (patient-provided): a TCGA-LUAD multi-omics set — gene-level copy number, RNA-seq,
somatic mutations (MAF), TMB, MSigDB Hallmark ssGSEA scores, and an **RPA (RTK/RAS/RAF Pathway
Alteration) classification** giving 118 driver-negative vs 383 driver-positive cases.

## 2. Methodology and the Asta agents used

We ran a full **generate → stress-test → validate** loop, deliberately trying to *break* each positive
finding rather than confirm it.

| Asta agent | Role in this project |
|---|---|
| **Theorizer** | Generated the founding CIN → immune-escape → TIGIT/LAG3 hypothesis. |
| **Paper Finder** (`asta literature find`) | 8 literature searches — a structured gap analysis (615 unique papers) + mechanism/biology grounding. Established that the *specific* junction (CIN ↔ TIGIT/LAG3 in driver-negative LUAD) was an unstudied white space. |
| **AutoDiscovery (AutoDS)** | 6 runs / 120 autonomous experiments — Bayesian-surprise-guided hypothesis testing across the five gaps (association, driver-specificity, checkpoint selectivity, cGAS-STING mediation, specific CN events), plus a decisive infiltration-normalized follow-up. |
| **DataVoyager** (`asta analyze-data`) | 5 targeted, code-executing validation analyses (E1, E2, E1b, E7-interaction) that re-tested the AutoDS leads under gold-standard metrics. |

We supplemented Asta with public reference data and a custom single-cell pipeline:
- **GDC PanCanAtlas** — Thorsson *leukocyte fraction* + CIBERSORT (independent CD8 abundance), Taylor
  **Aneuploidy Score** (purity-aware CIN), ABSOLUTE **purity/ploidy/WGD**; **TIMER2.0** (6-method CD8).
- **LuCA** core single-cell atlas (Salcher 2022; 892k cells) and a cohort-agnostic
  inferCNV → CD8-pseudobulk → tumor-level pipeline we built and unit-tested.

**A methodological principle we enforced throughout:** the independent unit is the **tumor**, not the
cell or the gene. Single-cell inference was done at tumor/patient level (pseudobulk), and we treated
tumor purity, CIN-metric choice, and infiltration quantification as first-class confounders.

## 4. What we learned

### About the biology of driver-negative LUAD
1. **The founding hypothesis is not supported.** CIN does **not** drive TIGIT/LAG3-mediated immune
   escape in driver-negative LUAD. The most enticing lead — a per-cell LAG3 up-regulation — was
   covariate-dependent in bulk and **did not replicate** when CD8 LAG3 was measured directly in
   single-cell data (β≈0). LAG3 blockade does not gain a rationale here from this dataset.
2. **Driver-negative LUAD is not a distinct CIN–immune compartment.** Whatever CIN does to the immune
   microenvironment, it does about equally in oncogene-positive tumors (no interaction). Framing CIN
   immunobiology as *specific* to the driver-negative subset is not warranted.
3. **What CIN association survives is immune "coldness," and much of even that is tumor purity.** The
   apparent CIN → reduced T-cell infiltration signal collapsed once we used a purity-corrected
   aneuploidy score and adjusted for ABSOLUTE purity. The one durable genomic correlate of coldness was
   **STK11 loss** — consistent with, not novel beyond, the known STK11/KEAP1 cold-tumor axis.

### Methodological lessons (these are the most transferable findings)
4. **CIN-metric choice changes the answer.** An expression/amplitude "fraction-genome-altered" proxy
   and the arm-level **Taylor Aneuploidy Score** are only moderately correlated (r≈0.6) and gave
   *opposite* significance. Bulk CIN–immunology conclusions built on FGA-type proxies should be
   treated with suspicion until reproduced with a purity-aware aneuploidy score.
5. **Tumor purity is a first-order confounder** in any bulk CIN↔immune analysis (it inflates
   proxy-CIN *and* dilutes the RNA immune signal). It must be modeled explicitly.
6. **"Per-cell" claims from bulk are fragile.** Adjusting a checkpoint for an expression-derived
   infiltration score (built from the same RNA) induces residual co-expression that can masquerade as
   a per-cell effect. Deconvolution disagreed with regression adjustment; single cell settled it.

### Practical / feasibility
7. **Driver-annotated single-cell LUAD does not exist at usable scale.** LuCA (the largest atlas) has
   driver annotation for only ~75/161 LUAD patients and just **one** eligible oncogene-negative primary
   tumor. Any single-cell interrogation of a *driver-negative-specific* mechanism is currently
   infeasible with public data — a real constraint for our subfield.

## 5. Implications for the driver-negative LUAD research program
- **Don't build a LAG3 (or TIGIT) therapeutic hypothesis for oncogene-negative LUAD on TCGA bulk
  checkpoint expression.** It doesn't survive rigorous re-analysis or single-cell validation.
- **Re-audit prior bulk CIN–immune claims** in this space with purity-corrected aneuploidy metrics and,
  where possible, single-cell or deconvolution — several may be purity/metric artifacts.
- **The interesting, still-open threads** are the ones that survived: (a) *what actually drives*
  oncogene-negative tumorigenesis (tumor-suppressor–loss/CIN biology), and (b) the **STK11-loss cold
  program** as a concrete, better-supported immune-evasion lead worth pursuing on its own.
- **If a CIN→LAG3 question is ever revisited**, it needs a driver-annotated single-cell or spatial
  cohort with LAG3 protein per T cell — data that would have to be generated, not mined.

## 6. What worked about the approach
The Asta loop was effective precisely because it produced a *falsifiable* lead fast (Theorizer →
AutoDS surfaced the per-cell LAG3 signal in hours) and then let us **kill it responsibly**: DataVoyager
re-tests exposed the metric/purity/covariate fragility, and the single-cell pipeline delivered the
clean negative. The headline deliverable is not a new drug target — it's a well-documented negative
result plus a set of methodological guardrails for bulk CIN-immunology, which is worth more than a
false-positive lead pursued into the wet lab.
